# Project Brief: DragonSword World & Lore Research

- Owner: repository operator
- Status: `ACTIVE`
- Goal: build a compact, evidence-led reference for DragonSword: Awakening that captures gameplay knowledge, canon lore, environmental clues, middle worldbuilding, and theories without confusing inference with fact.
- User/operator: BigCatMellow

## Current reality

- Checked facts:
  - repository was empty at bootstrap on 2026-09-21;
  - DragonSword has official Hound13/Steam material, in-game lore, developer Q&A, walkthroughs, community wikis, forums, and Reddit with uneven reliability;
  - Hound13 explicitly says some hidden lore exists in exploration and that world lore will continue expanding;
  - our conversation already produced useful gameplay notes, a middle-worldbuilding lens, an Orbis iceberg, and Organa/Kalagon theories, but some factual details came through secondary sources.
- Evidence/source paths:
  - [research/SOURCE-REGISTER.md](research/SOURCE-REGISTER.md)
  - [research/CLAIM-LEDGER.md](research/CLAIM-LEDGER.md)
- Important assumptions:
  - English localization may contain awkward wording;
  - community resources may repeat one another or invent unsupported lore;
  - direct playthrough evidence will eventually replace secondary reports for important claims.

## Definition of DONE

For the current bootstrap:

- Finished result: a fresh agent can open the repository, understand its rules and research lens, distinguish canon from inference, find our existing conclusions, and know the next unanswered questions without relying on chat history.
- Final proof:
  - README routes to every canonical owner;
  - claim ledger separates VERIFIED / REPORTED / INFERRED / SPECULATIVE / UNKNOWN;
  - current conversation findings are captured in their proper owners;
  - source register records provenance and source quality;
  - roadmap identifies next evidence work.
- Final proof performed/inspected by: repository operator.

The wider lore project is intentionally ongoing as the player advances and Hound13 adds content.

## Scope and boundaries

- In scope:
  - gameplay reference notes;
  - story/lore facts;
  - environmental storytelling;
  - art-direction/worldbuilding analysis;
  - historical, social, economic, religious, and institutional inference;
  - competing lore theories;
  - source/evidence tracking.
- Not doing:
  - datamining unreleased content;
  - extracting proprietary assets;
  - pretending community lore is canon;
  - exhaustive build/tier-list maintenance unless useful to the player's current playthrough;
  - copying the full MAPS_L runtime/control plane into a research notebook.
- Effort limit: keep the common reading path to README + one relevant owner document + claim/source ledger when evidence is needed.

## Constraints and quality bar

- Evidence outranks remembered summaries.
- Primary source preferred.
- Every major theory must expose missing links.
- Strong theories include at least one plausible competing explanation.
- Concision over encyclopedia sprawl.
- Spoilers should be signposted.
- One concept, one owner document.

## Unknowns and risks

- Exact primary-source wording for much of the in-game mythology is not yet captured.
- Several community sites appear confident while lacking transparent sourcing.
- Localization can make metaphysical terms sound more definite than intended.
- Research/prototype needed first: direct screenshots/transcripts/codex entries for the highest-leverage lore claims.
- Evidence that would invalidate the current plan: Hound13 publishes a complete official lore reference that supersedes secondary reconstruction.

## Decision path

- Owner may decide: document organization, source grading, claim confidence, theory structure, routine research and commits.
- Escalate to operator: material expansion into another project, destructive repository changes, paid research/resources, or publication beyond the existing public GitHub repository.
