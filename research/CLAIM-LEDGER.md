# Claim Ledger

Canonical owner for claim status. Analysis files may explain claims but should not independently redefine their evidence status.

## Status key

- **VERIFIED** — direct game/official evidence.
- **REPORTED** — secondary source reports the game/quest detail; direct check pending.
- **INFERRED** — reasoned consequence of evidence.
- **SPECULATIVE** — plausible theory with material missing links.
- **UNKNOWN** — unresolved question.

## Important discipline

The ledger records **epistemic status**, not how interesting a claim is.

A compelling theory remains SPECULATIVE until evidence closes the missing link.
