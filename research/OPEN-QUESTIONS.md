## Player fieldwork protocol

When something seems odd:

1. screenshot it if possible;
2. record quest/chapter/location;
3. quote exact text rather than memory;
4. note who is speaking and what they could know;
5. distinguish statue/item/environment from dialogue;
6. ask what alternative explanation fits;
7. update the claim ledger only after checking evidence.
