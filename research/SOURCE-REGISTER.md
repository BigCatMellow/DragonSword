## Source handling rules

### Primary preferred

Best evidence:

1. direct in-game text / screenshot / environmental observation;
2. Hound13 official page/announcement/video;
3. developer interview/Q&A.

### Secondary use

Walkthroughs are useful when they document an exact quest event that we have not personally rechecked. Mark resulting claims **REPORTED**, not VERIFIED.

### Community use

Community wikis can surface names, quests, item descriptions, and connections worth checking. Treat unsourced statements as leads.

### Localization

If English wording makes a metaphysical claim unusually strong — e.g. “god,” “demon,” “resurrection,” “origin,” “curse” — preserve the wording and, when important, compare other localization/original-language evidence before building a large theory on one word.

## Highest-value evidence gap

Direct capture of the relevant in-game lines for:

- Organa defeating Kalagon;
- what Kalagon actually is called;
- origin and uses of Dragon Dust;
- Origin Dragon definition;
- Six Heroes monument / missing sixth;
- Ewald's account of Ysera, Ropie, Arugal and the Dragon Dust;
- Organa Order's own account versus non-Order accounts.
