# DragonSword Research Operating Contract

This repository is a durable knowledge system for DragonSword: Awakening. Use the smallest amount of process that keeps lore analysis trustworthy and easy to continue.

## Authority

1. This file defines repository-wide research rules.
2. [PROJECT_BRIEF.md](PROJECT_BRIEF.md) and [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) define approved project scope and direction.
3. Game/official developer evidence outranks secondary summaries.
4. Research notes and theories never become authority merely because they are written confidently.

Capability is not permission. Do not expand this repository into unrelated game tooling, datamining, or publication projects without explicit scope.

## Evidence before prose

For material lore claims:

- inspect current evidence when practical;
- prefer Hound13, official Steam material, direct game text, screenshots, codex entries, quest dialogue, and environmental evidence;
- use secondary walkthroughs/wikis as leads or reported evidence, not automatic canon;
- preserve disagreements and uncertainty;
- never fill a missing link because it makes a theory cleaner.

Use these labels consistently:

- **VERIFIED**
- **REPORTED**
- **INFERRED**
- **SPECULATIVE**
- **UNKNOWN**

When a claim changes status, update its canonical owner in [research/CLAIM-LEDGER.md](research/CLAIM-LEDGER.md).

## Source hierarchy

Default order:

1. direct in-game evidence;
2. Hound13 / official DragonSword material;
3. official developer interviews/Q&A;
4. reliable walkthrough/video showing the relevant game text;
5. community wikis/guides;
6. Reddit/forum recollection;
7. unsourced memory.

A lower source can surface a lead. It cannot silently overrule stronger evidence.

## Research method

For important questions:

**question → sources → claims/evidence → inference → competing explanation → confidence → open question**

Separate observation from interpretation.

Example:

- VERIFIED/REPORTED: the Six Heroes monument depicts five heroes.
- INFERRED: one hero has been intentionally excluded from public commemoration.
- SPECULATIVE: the exclusion reflects deliberate political historical revision.
- UNKNOWN: who ordered the removal, when, and why.

## Worldbuilding method

When examining the setting, use three scales:

- **Macro** — history, cosmology, wars, major powers.
- **Middle** — institutions, economics, jobs, law, infrastructure, customs, ordinary adaptation.
- **Micro** — specific people, places, objects, local stories.

Apply the **write-the-sequel** test:

**premise → consequence → adaptation → normalization → culture → present-day story**

Do not assume realism is the goal. Ask what the art direction and environmental design communicate about lived experience.

## Documentation

Prefer consolidation over accumulation.

- one concept, one owner document;
- link rather than copy;
- claim status lives in the claim ledger;
- source metadata lives in the source register;
- new theory files need a distinct question that cannot fit an existing owner;
- keep navigation short.

## Spoilers

Mark major story spoilers. Do not erase spoiler material merely because it is late-game; isolate it clearly.

## Verification

This is primarily a research notebook, so default risk is low:

- owner verifies structure, links, and claim labels;
- consequential theory revisions should include at least one competing explanation;
- unsupported claims remain UNKNOWN/SPECULATIVE rather than being “resolved” by confidence.

## Completion reporting

For substantive updates, summarize:

**Changed:** material knowledge added/revised  
**Evidence:** strongest new source or observation  
**Changed confidence:** theories promoted/demoted  
**Open:** highest-value unresolved question
