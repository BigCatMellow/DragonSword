# Puzzles

Puzzle-only lookup. Use the lightest hint first.

## Chapter 1 — Bookshelf

**Hint 1:** The room itself hides the route.

<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>

The shelves are not all fixed scenery.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Push the far-left bookshelf.

</details>

## Chapter 1 — Six platforms

**Hint 1:** Count your temporary party.

<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>

Every plate must be occupied simultaneously.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Use all six party members on the six plates.

</details>

## Chapter 2 — Dragon altar

**Hint 1:** You are missing something visible only after changing the room.

<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>

Inspect cracked walls.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Break the cracked wall to reveal another pillar, then place the pillars on their platforms.

</details>

## Chapter 5 — Falling floor

**Hint 1:** The panel edges matter.

<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>

Try the seam.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Walk the central seam between the falling panels.

</details>

## Chapter 6 — Mural door

**Hint 1:** You already saw the solution.

<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>

Earlier mural.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Reproduce the earlier mural's arrangement.

</details>

## Chapter 6 — Prison symbols

**Hint 1:** Backtrack one room.

<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>

Look at walls and ceiling.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Match the prison switches to the three symbols from the previous lab room.

</details>

## Chapter 7 — Skyridge ruins

**Hint 1:** Missing parts are nearby.

<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>

One low, one high.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Find the ground switch and the switch on the broken pillar, install both, then use the mural.

</details>

## Chapter 7 — Underwater symbols

**Hint 1:** One clue is across; one is below.

<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>

Check the waterline.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Solve the opposite-side pillar, then the partly submerged symbol below.

</details>

## Chapter 8 — Electric Core

**Hint 1:** It is part of the boss mechanic.

<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>

Electricity charges it.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Use electric attacks or bait electric boss attacks into the Core.

</details>
