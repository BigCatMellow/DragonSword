## Just below

**Mercenary economy**  
Professional companies imply contracts, clients, reputation, pricing, competition and security gaps.

**Uneven state control**  
If mercenaries routinely solve dangerous local problems, royal protection probably weakens away from major centers.

**Monster adaptation**  
A world accustomed to monsters should have escort culture, guard work, route knowledge, warnings and practical folklore.

## Institutional layer

**Crown**  
Knight orders imply recruitment, command, logistics and regional responsibility.

**Organa Order**  
Potential overlap of religion, law, sacred knowledge and Dragon-related authority.

**Trading power**  
Continent-scale commerce naturally creates logistics, intelligence and political leverage.

**Magic**  
If magic is professionalized, there should be education, regulation, military use and specialized jobs.

## Historical sediment

The 60-year peace should leave:

- veterans
- reconstruction
- memorials
- inherited stories
- arguments over what really happened

The phrase **Six Heroes** surviving beside a reported five-person monument is especially suspicious.

## Deep layer

Dragon Dust may connect:

**religion ↔ history ↔ economy ↔ power ↔ danger ↔ Dragons**

That makes it one of the best lenses for understanding the world.
