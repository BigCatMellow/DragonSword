# Organa and Kalagon

Current theory page. **Not canon unless marked clearly.**

## Reported foundation

- Organa defeated Kalagon.
- Kalagon's remains are tied to Dragon Dust.
- Dragon Dust can empower humans.
- Kalagon's remains appear generative as well as dangerous.
- an Arcana description reportedly says a dragon **“rebelled against the Goddess.”**

That last word matters.

**Rebelled** suggests a prior relationship/order, not merely an outside monster arriving.

## Theory A — orthodox version

Organa = benevolent goddess  
Kalagon = destructive Dragon  
Organa saves the world

Still plausible.

## Theory B — victory created the next problem

Organa defeats Kalagon  
→ remains persist  
→ Dragon Dust enters civilization  
→ people use Dragon power  
→ later Dragon crises inherit that power

This currently fits many clues, but key links still need primary evidence.

## Theory C — Kalagon as rebel

Possible:

Organa's order  
→ Kalagon rejects/rebels against it  
→ Great War  
→ Organa wins  
→ Kalagon remembered as Dark/Demon Dragon

This does **not** prove he was morally right.

## Generative-body clue

Kalagon's body appears capable of producing/empowering life rather than merely causing rot or destruction.

That weakens a simplistic symbolic reading of:

**Organa = life / Kalagon = death**

A possible polarity is:

**Organa = order / stability**  
**Kalagon = change / generative power / dangerous freedom**

Still speculative.

## Could Kalagon have been a hero?

Possible **from a dragon/follower perspective**.

Not enough evidence yet to say:

> Kalagon was secretly the good guy.

## Could the current Dragon be one of the Six?

Mechanically/thematically possible because:

- five Heroes reportedly took Dragon Dust;
- Ropie took Arugal's heart;
- human/Dragon transformation appears possible in this world.

Strong theory to test, not established identity.

## Best questions

- What exactly did Kalagon rebel against?
- Who tells the oldest version of the myth?
- What do Dragons say about Kalagon?
- What did Ropie know?
- Does Dragon Dust transform, merely empower, or both?
