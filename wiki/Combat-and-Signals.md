# Combat and Signals

## Core idea

Your party is not three separate characters.

Think:

> **one moveset spread across three bodies**

Good combat means switching often.

## Basic loop

**attack → apply condition/status → Signal activates → switch → Signal Skill → next trigger**

The exact conditions depend on the heroes.

Common triggers include:

- Bleed
- Poison
- Airborne
- Knockdown
- Stun
- Break

## Team-building question

Do not ask only:

> Who has the highest attack?

Ask:

> What does Hero A cause?

Then:

> Does Hero B have a Signal that reacts to that?

Then:

> What does B cause that C can exploit?

Ideal shape:

**A → B → C → A**

Not every team completes a perfect loop, but the closer it gets, the smoother combat feels.

## Super Armor

The enemy's white bar is a separate problem from HP.

### While white bar is active

Many enemies resist/ignore stronger control effects.

### After Break

You get the better window for:

- crowd control;
- Signal chains;
- burst damage;
- ultimates / long cooldowns.

Simple rhythm:

**pressure → Break → chain → burst**

## Status effects

Damage-over-time/status effects can still matter before Break.

Control effects become much more valuable after Super Armor is gone.

## Switch-cancel / overlap trick

Some hero attacks have long animations.

Try:

1. launch the long attack;
2. switch before it finishes;
3. begin the next hero's action.

The first hero may continue their animation while the new hero acts.

That creates real overlap.

## Practical combat checklist

If a boss feels too tanky:

- Are you breaking the white bar?
- Are you triggering Signals?
- Are you switching often enough?
- Are all 3 heroes leveled?
- Is Karma equipped?
- Are skill levels behind?
- Are you dumping burst before Break?
