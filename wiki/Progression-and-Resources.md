# Progression and Resources

## Hero Level

Use **Combat Texts**.

Random enemy XP is not the intended main leveling route.

Early goal:

**main 3 at similar levels**

rather than one overleveled carry.

## Breakthrough

Raises level cap.

If:

**Level X — MAX**

check Breakthrough before assuming you need more XP.

## Skills

Separate from hero level.

Prioritize:

- frequently used skills;
- important Signal Skills;
- core damage/control tools.

Do not spend equally just because the menu allows it.

## Karma

One of the biggest hidden progression systems.

Think:

**Karma = major power/equipment slot**

Do:

- equip it;
- level it;
- upgrade/replace it as stronger options appear.

A missing/underleveled Karma can make a properly leveled hero feel weak.

## Gear

Early:

- equip useful gear;
- modest upgrades;
- keep moving.

Do not spend heavily trying to perfect early drops.

Later, gear becomes more build-specific.

Possible later pattern:

**3-piece set + 2-piece set**

can be better than blindly forcing all 5 from one set.

Patch/build dependent.

## Awakening

Not just tiny stats.

Awakening can change or improve how a hero functions.

Early:

**save deeper investment for heroes you actually like/use**

## Resource activities

When you need something:

1. open the item/material source info;
2. check dungeon/resource activity reward tables;
3. farm the targeted activity;
4. avoid random-world grinding unless the item specifically drops there.

## World bosses

Worth doing when available.

Useful for progression resources, including Karma-related rewards.

## Cooking

Keep ingredients.

Food can cover:

- healing;
- buffs;
- damage;
- revival.

Harder content becomes easier when you actually use consumables.

## If progress stops

Check:

**story progress → Mercenary progression → Breakthrough → material requirement**

not just XP.

## If damage feels low

Check roughly:

**hero level → Breakthrough → Karma → skill levels → gear → team synergy**
