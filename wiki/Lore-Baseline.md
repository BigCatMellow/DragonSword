# Lore Baseline

A spoiler-aware summary of what we currently trust most.

## High confidence

- Orbis is presented as the first kingdom built by humankind.
- A Dragon once brought the continent near destruction.
- Six Heroes saved the world.
- Roughly 60 years later, a Dragon threat has returned.
- Hound13 deliberately hides lore in exploration/environmental details.

## Important reported clues

Direct in-game capture still needed for some of these:

- the Six Heroes monument reportedly depicts only five;
- Ropie is publicly remembered as “the traitor”;
- Organa defeated Kalagon;
- Kalagon's remains are tied to Dragon Dust;
- Dragon Dust can grant extraordinary power but has dangerous consequences;
- five Heroes reportedly consumed Dragon Dust to defeat Arugal;
- Ropie refused it but took Arugal's heart.

## Do not assume yet

Still theory/unknown:

- Kalagon was secretly good;
- Organa was secretly evil;
- the returning Dragon is definitely one of the Six;
- every Origin Dragon is literally a piece of Kalagon;
- the Dragon crises are a proven repeating cycle.
