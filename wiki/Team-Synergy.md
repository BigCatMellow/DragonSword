# Team Synergy

Synergy matters more than simply choosing three individually strong heroes.

> **Main rule: build around Signal triggers.**

## How to evaluate a team

For each hero:

1. what status/control effect do they cause?
2. what activates their Signal Skills?
3. what do their Signal Skills cause?
4. can that trigger another hero?

Best-case structure:

**Hero A triggers B → B triggers C → C helps reset/trigger A**

## Easy early-game principle

A good beginner team usually wants:

- one reliable setup/status source;
- one hero who responds to it;
- one sustain/control/flexible slot.

Do not chase a perfect endgame composition at level 10.

## Do not copy teams blindly

A team from a guide can feel bad if:

- your Signal conditions are not unlocked/upgraded;
- your Karma/gear is behind;
- you do not understand the intended opener;
- a patch changed values;
- the composition assumes later Awakening.

## Quick test for your current team

Open each hero's skills.

Write:

**A causes:** ___  
**B activates from:** ___  
**B causes:** ___  
**C activates from:** ___

If the blanks connect naturally, the team probably has real synergy.

If nothing connects, you are mostly playing three separate characters.
