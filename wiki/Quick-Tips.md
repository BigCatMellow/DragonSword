# Quick Tips

For “I just want the useful stuff.”

- Combat Texts = normal leveling.
- Don't grind random enemies for XP.
- Level your main 3 fairly evenly.
- MAX level early? Check **Breakthrough**.
- Equip **Karma** on everyone.
- Skill levels are separate.
- Check Signal triggers before judging a hero.
- Build teams where **A triggers B → B triggers C**.
- White bar = Super Armor. Break it before your big combo.
- Start long attack → switch early → previous hero may keep attacking.
- Unlock dungeon entrances when you find them.
- Check reward lists before farming random mobs.
- Nearby puzzles/chests are usually worth doing.
- Unreachable area may require a later traversal ability.
- Cooking is useful; keep ingredients.
- Mounts/familiars are not just cosmetic.
- Story/Mercenary progression can block upgrades even when you have XP.
- Early gear: good enough > perfect.
- Awakening can change how heroes play.
- If suddenly weak: **level → Breakthrough → Karma → skills → gear → synergy**.
- Screenshot murals/patterns in ruins. The game reuses visual clues.
- When stuck, check **up/down elevation** before assuming a bug.
