# Things Easy to Miss

A quick list of systems DragonSword does not explain especially well.

## Character leveling

**Combat Texts are normal leveling.**

Do not save them like rare candies.

Random enemy XP is tiny compared with using Combat Texts.

Early rule:

**pick 3 → level those 3 fairly evenly**

Avoid spreading resources across every hero at once.

---

## Breakthrough

If a hero says **MAX** before you expect:

**check Breakthrough**

It raises the level cap.

---

## Karma

Easy to overlook. Very important.

Think of Karma as roughly:

**major equipment / weapon-like power system**

Do:

- equip Karma on all 3 main heroes;
- level it;
- replace weak/general Karma later;
- do not leave the slot empty.

If you suddenly feel much weaker than content expects, Karma is one of the first things to check.

---

## Skill levels are separate

Hero level does **not** automatically level skills.

Upgrade the abilities you actually use.

---

## Signal Skills are the combat system

Do not think of the party as:

**main character + 2 backups**

Think:

**3 heroes = one combined moveset**

Check each hero's Signal conditions.

Goal:

**A triggers B → B triggers C**

---

## The white bar matters

White bar = **Super Armor**.

Against tougher enemies:

**break white bar → use control/status/Signal chain → burst damage**

Do not just stare at HP.

---

## Switching can overlap attacks

Some long attacks keep playing after you switch.

Useful pattern:

**start long animation → switch early → both characters contribute**

This is one of the game's less obvious combat tricks.

---

## Exploration feeds progression

Do not treat chests/puzzles as pure collectibles.

Useful exploration can feed:

- upgrade materials;
- treasure maps;
- Master Skill-related progression;
- Awakening-related progression;
- traversal unlocks;
- fast travel.

If something looks inaccessible, it may require a later traversal ability rather than a hidden path.

---

## Unlock dungeon entrances

Even if you do not run them immediately.

Later:

**need material → check dungeon rewards → fast travel back**

Much easier than rediscovering the location.

---

## Check reward lists

When you need an item:

**check dungeon/resource activity rewards first**

Do not immediately grind random enemies.

---

## Cooking is useful

Food can provide:

- healing;
- buffs;
- damage help;
- revival.

Do not assume ingredients are junk.

---

## Mounts / familiars are more than cosmetics

Collect them.

They can matter for:

- traversal;
- access to places;
- broader progression/bonuses.

---

## Mercenary progression ≠ hero level

Your heroes have their own progression.

Your broader Mercenary/story progression controls other unlocks and ceilings.

If leveling/progression suddenly stops, the problem may be:

**story / Mercenary rank / Breakthrough**

not XP.

---

## Early-game priority

1. continue main story until systems open
2. settle on 3 heroes
3. level all 3
4. learn Signal conditions
5. equip Karma
6. upgrade useful skills
7. learn Break windows
8. unlock travel + dungeon entrances
9. grab nearby puzzles/chests
10. worry about serious gear optimization later
