# Puzzle Index — Hints First

This page collects environmental puzzles without the surrounding story.

## Chapter 1 — Bookshelf

**Hint 1:** The room itself is hiding the route.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The shelves are not all fixed scenery.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

After clearing the room, push the far-left bookshelf to reveal the hidden passage.

</details>

---

## Chapter 1 — Six platforms

**Hint 1:** Count the people currently available to you.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The puzzle expects every pressure plate to be occupied at the same time.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Use the combined Johnny + Red Fox party to occupy all six plates; the player uses the designated lower-right plate.

</details>

---

## Chapter 2 — Dragon altar

**Hint 1:** Not every required object is visible when you first enter.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Look for damaged architecture as well as movable pillars.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Use the first pillar to progress, break the cracked wall to expose another pillar, then use the newly revealed pillar/platform combinations to open the remaining path.

</details>

---

## Chapter 5 — Falling floor

**Hint 1:** The floor reacts to where your feet actually touch.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Look at the boundaries between the panels rather than treating each tile as fully unsafe.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Move along the central seam between the unstable panels to reach the lever.

</details>

---

## Chapter 6 — Mural door

**Hint 1:** The solution was shown before the lock.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The earlier mural is not decoration.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Reproduce the earlier mural's arrangement at the later door mechanism. Take a screenshot of the first mural when you see it.

</details>

---

## Chapter 6 — Brazier / pots

**Hint 1:** The nearby mural tells you which positions matter.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

You need two active positions, not all three.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Use the top-left and bottom pressure positions; leave the top position inactive.

</details>

---

## Chapter 6 — Prison symbols

**Hint 1:** The symbols are not hidden in the prison room itself.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Backtrack mentally one room. Check walls **and ceiling**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Match the three rotating prison switches to the three symbols shown in the previous lab room.

</details>

---

## Chapter 7 — First Skyridge ruins

**Hint 1:** The door is missing physical parts.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

One is easy to spot. For the other, look upward near the broken stonework.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Find one switch on the ground and another atop the broken pillar left of the descending route. Install both and match the mural.

</details>

---

## Chapter 7 — Second ruins / underwater symbols

**Hint 1:** The two door lights correspond to two separate nearby symbol mechanisms.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

One clue is across the gap. The other requires looking below the waterline.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Solve the opposite-side pillar first, then drop to the lower water-covered level and match the partly submerged symbol for the second light.

</details>

---

## Chapter 8 — Electric Core

**Hint 1:** Treat the Core as part of the fight, not scenery.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

It wants electricity.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Charge the Core with electric attacks / bait electric boss attacks into it to trigger the overload and break the barrier.

</details>
