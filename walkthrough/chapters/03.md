## Teleport

**Symptom:** interacted with the worship object, got moved somewhere else, and it feels like the quest broke.

**Hint 1:** The relocation is the progression.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Do not try to return to where the object was.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The teleport is scripted. Continue deeper through the Dragon Cult interior and follow the new objective.

</details>

## If objectives seem to jump

Chapter 3 uses scripted state changes. Trust the **newest objective marker/text**, not the place where the previous objective started.
