## Fog road

**Symptom:** the northern road appears to simply end.

**Hint 1:** The road is blocked; the route is not.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Look vertically beside the obstruction.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Climb the adjacent cliff and continue along the upper route.

</details>

## Reina rescue

**Symptom:** looking for a separate Lich target and cannot find it.

**Hint 1:** You may be searching for a fight the quest does not actually require.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Follow the rescue objective/state rather than the enemy name you expect.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The documented route does not require a separate Lich hunt. Continue the Reina rescue sequence and follow the updated marker.

</details>

## Falling floor

**Hint 1:** The unsafe tiles have boundaries.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The seam matters more than the center of each panel.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Walk carefully along the central seam between falling panels to reach the lever.

</details>
