# Chapter 7 — Hint Guide

## Alboin

**Symptom:** searching the city for Alboin and cannot find him.

**Hint 1:** Check whether the story state has already moved past the search.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

If a palace briefing has become active, that is now the correct objective.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Go to the palace meeting when the briefing begins. Alboin appears there as part of the story sequence.

</details>

## First ruins

**Symptom:** door mechanism is missing switches/components.

**Hint 1:** Both missing pieces are near the entrance.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

One is low. One is high.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Pick up the switch on the ground, then climb the broken pillar to the left of the descending route for the second. Install both and match the mural.

</details>

## Second ruins

**Hint 1:** Two lights, two nearby symbol solutions.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Search across the gap for one. Search below the waterline for the other.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Solve the opposite-side pillar symbol, then drop to the lower water-covered area and match the partly submerged symbol to light the second lantern/open the door.

</details>

## General Chapter 7 rule

The story increasingly uses **briefings and objective-state changes**. If an NPC seems missing, verify that the active story phase has not relocated them before searching the previous area.
