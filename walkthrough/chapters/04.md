## Barrier Stone

**Symptom:** quest wording makes it sound like you are missing a material/ingredient.

**Hint 1:** Do not start farming.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The story has already prepared what you need.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Follow Karina/Bardok's story route and use the prepared Barrier Stone interaction. There is no unnamed ingredient you need to grind.

</details>

## If the next route feels unclear

After reporting the restoration, follow the newly assigned northern investigation rather than continuing to search around the Barrier Stone area.
