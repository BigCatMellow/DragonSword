## Webbed Eona tower

**Hint 1:** The obstruction is a material clue.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Think fire/explosion rather than cutting.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Use Aria's bomb/fire-capable attack on the cobwebs, then interact with Eona's Legacy.

</details>

## Ancient device

**Symptom:** found and installed the gear, but nothing seems to happen.

**Hint 1:** Do not leave the machine yet.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Installing the part is only the first interaction.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

After installing the recovered gear-shaped component, interact with the additional control on the device.

</details>

## Dragon altar

**Hint 1:** If you seem to have too few movable pillars, trust that feeling.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Inspect damaged walls.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Use the visible pillar to progress, break the cracked wall to reveal a hidden pillar, then move the pillars onto the corresponding pressure platforms to open the remaining chambers.

</details>

## Good habit for this chapter

When a ruin shows you a mural, unusual wall pattern, symbol layout or machine state, screenshot it. Later puzzles begin reusing environmental clues instead of putting the key beside the lock.
