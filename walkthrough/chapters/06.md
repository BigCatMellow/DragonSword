# Chapter 6 — Hint Guide

Back: [Walkthrough home](../README.md)

> This chapter begins exposing major Six Heroes lore. Hints below avoid explaining the revelations unless needed for progression.

## Ropi cabin

**Symptom:** reached the cabin but the sequence does not seem to advance.

**Hint 1:** Clear the immediate area first, then treat the cabin as an investigation space.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The letter has a place inside.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Clear the monsters around the cabin, enter, place Ewald's letter at the visible interaction point and inspect/continue the scene.

</details>

## Mural door

**Symptom:** later ruin door has no obvious switch.

**Hint 1:** The lock's key was shown earlier.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Think visually, not numerically. Remember the earlier mural.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Reproduce the arrangement shown by the earlier mural at the door mechanism.

</details>

## Braziers

**Hint 1:** The mural gives a positional answer.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Not every platform should be active.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Activate the top-left and bottom positions; leave the top position inactive.

</details>

## Prison symbols

**Hint 1:** The answer is outside the prison room.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Look back in the previous lab room, including above eye level.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

</details>

## Tomb platforms

**Hint 1:** The environment is showing you an order.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Treat the mural as instructions rather than decoration.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Use the mural's depicted platform sequence. At the entrance, the useful route begins with the lower/adjacent platforms rather than the top one.

</details>
