## Fallen tree

**Hint 1:** This is teaching an interaction state, not asking you to attack it.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Interact with the tree first. Movement becomes the action.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Interact with the fallen tree, then move forward to push it away.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

</details>
