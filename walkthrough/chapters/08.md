# Chapter 8 — Hint Guide

Back: [Walkthrough home](../README.md)

> **ENDING SPOILERS.** Use only when you are in Chapter 8.

## Dragon nest

**Symptom:** first nest route contains broken eggs.

**Hint 1:** The failed nest is information, not a dead end.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Look for evidence of something large moving through the area.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Follow the unusually large Worm tunnel toward the intact nest.

</details>

## Stolen egg

**Symptom:** Worm steals the egg; unsure whether to keep searching the nest.

**Hint 1:** Chase the event, not the original plan.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The objective will eventually relocate away from the nest.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Follow the Worm through the large tunnel, clear only what blocks pursuit, then leave when the active objective moves back to the surface/operation area. The failed bait plan is scripted.

</details>

## Boss moves

**Symptom:** final boss leaves the arena.

**Hint 1:** The fight is not over.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Watch the new objective marker after every retreat.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Follow the relocated objective each time. The encounter deliberately moves between areas.

</details>

## Electric Core

**Hint 1:** The Core is a combat mechanic.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

It stores the same kind of energy the boss uses.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Charge the Electric Core with electric attacks or bait electric boss attacks into it to trigger the overload/barrier-breaking effect.

</details>
