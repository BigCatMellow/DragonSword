# Chapter 1 — Hint Guide

Back: [Walkthrough home](../README.md)

## Goblin cave

**Symptom:** cave entrance blocked by a large rock.

**Hint 1:** You do not need a new skill or later quest item.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Look at the objects beside the obstruction. One reacts badly to damage.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Use/shoot the explosive barrels beside the rock to open the cave.

</details>

## Bookshelf

**Symptom:** cleared the room but cannot find the route onward.

**Hint 1:** One part of the room is movable.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Check the bookshelf line.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Push the far-left bookshelf to expose the hidden doorway.

</details>

## Six platforms

**Hint 1:** This is a head-count puzzle.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The temporary alliance is part of the solution.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Get all six party members onto the six pressure plates; use the designated lower-right plate with your controlled character.

</details>

## If the chapter seems finished but will not advance

Finish the cave encounter and follow the return objective toward Orbis. Do not stay searching the cave for an extra progression trigger unless the objective still points there.
