# Lore Baseline

A compact answer to: **what does the world currently appear to establish?**

Use [the claim ledger](../research/CLAIM-LEDGER.md) for epistemic status.

## Premise

- **VERIFIED — C001:** Orbis is described by Hound13 as the first kingdom built by humankind.
- **VERIFIED — C002:** a Dragon once brought the continent close to destruction; six Heroes saved it; 60 years later a Dragon threat has returned.
- **VERIFIED — C004:** the game deliberately frames cooking, local problems, exploration and relationships as part of living in Orbis rather than mere side systems.
- Lute enters this world from a small-scale mercenary perspective rather than from its political/religious elite.

## The Six Heroes problem

### Public layer

The culture remembers **Six Heroes** as the saviors of the prior Dragon crisis.

### Cracks in that layer

- **REPORTED — C010:** the monument to the Six Heroes depicts only five.
- **REPORTED — C015:** Ropie is remembered as “the traitor.”
- **VERIFIED — S001:** Hound13 explicitly says tracing the old Heroes will uncover their hidden secrets.

Working conclusion:

> The heroic summary is useful public history, not sufficient historical evidence.

See [C023-C024](../research/CLAIM-LEDGER.md).

## Kalagon / Organa / Dragon Dust

Currently strongest reported chain:

**Organa defeats Kalagon**  
→ **Kalagon's body becomes a source of Dragon Dust**  
→ **Dragon Dust becomes a valuable/useful material**  
→ **Dragon Dust can grant extraordinary power but carries severe danger**  
→ **the prior Heroes become entangled with that power during the Arugal crisis**

Status: important but still mostly **REPORTED**, because our present evidence comes through a detailed quest walkthrough rather than direct captured game text.

See C011-C014.

## Origin Dragons

Current reported evidence suggests “Origin Dragon” is a meaningful in-world category, not just a generic description.

- **REPORTED — C016:** Largo identifies as an Origin Dragon and speaks of slain dragons as brothers.
- Relationship among Kalagon, Dragon Dust, Dragon remains and Origin Dragons still requires primary verification.

Do not yet simplify this to “all dragons are pieces of Kalagon.”

## Institutions visible around the story

The world clearly contains competing interests rather than one unified “good kingdom.”

Recurring groups in the current playthrough/conversation include:

- Orbis royal power / knight orders
- Organa religious authority
- mercenary companies
- merchant/trading power
- rebels/opposition groups
- magic/alchemical specialists
- archaeologists/explorers

Specific jurisdiction, history and faction relationships remain research targets.

## What Hound13 says about hidden lore

**VERIFIED — C003:** Hound13 says hidden lore exists in exploration and that world lore will expand.

This matters methodologically:

> A ruin, statue, road, watchtower, abandoned structure, item description or ordinary profession may be evidence, not decoration.

The large blank between the ancient myth and Six Heroes era is one of the project's highest-value research spaces.

## What we should not claim yet

Not currently established strongly enough:

- Organa and Kalagon's origins;
- exact cosmology;
- whether Kalagon is literally a “demon” in a metaphysical sense or only called that in religious language/localization;
- whether Organa and Kalagon are equal/opposite beings;
- whether the Dragon crises are a literal repeating cycle;
- whether all Origin Dragons come directly from Kalagon;
- whether Ropie's “traitor” label is justified;
- whether ancient ruins/Eona infrastructure belong to a civilization predating current Orbis;
- whether humans can literally transform into Dragons.

Those belong in theory/unknown space until better evidence arrives.
