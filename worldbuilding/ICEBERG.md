# The Orbis Iceberg

A layered reconstruction of what the visible world implies.

Status labels come from [the claim ledger](../research/CLAIM-LEDGER.md).

## Layer 0 — visible surface

Mostly VERIFIED/REPORTED:

- Orbis: first human kingdom — **C001**
- prior Dragon crisis / Six Heroes / 60 years — **C002**
- hidden lore exists in exploration — **C003**
- ordinary-life framing — **C004**
- public Six Heroes story has a missing-monument problem — **C010**
- Kalagon / Organa / Dragon Dust chain — **C011-C013**
- Ysera/Ropie/Arugal dispute — **C014-C015**
- Origin Dragon category — **C016**

## Layer 1 — everyday systems

### Mercenary economy — **INFERRED C020**

Professional mercenary groups imply:

- contracts;
- clients;
- reputation;
- competition;
- prices;
- information brokers;
- repeat demand for armed problem-solving.

### Uneven security — **INFERRED C021**

If mercenaries and local residents routinely solve dangerous problems, centralized protection is probably uneven.

Conceptual model:

```text
capital / castle      ██████████
major roads           ███████░░░
settlements           █████░░░░░
remote countryside    ███░░░░░░░
wilderness            █░░░░░░░░░
```

Not literal data; a way to think about where non-state actors become useful.

### Monster adaptation — INFERRED

A monster-filled world should produce ordinary adaptations:

- escort culture;
- guard work;
- route knowledge;
- stronger storage/fences;
- regional warnings;
- hunting professions;
- folklore based on practical danger.

## Layer 2 — institutions

### Crown

Numbered/specialized knight forces imply administration behind the visible heroes:

- recruitment;
- command structure;
- logistics;
- training;
- armories;
- regional responsibilities.

### Organa Order

If the Order can define heresy, use armed agents and handle Dragon-related knowledge/materials, it may function partly like a second jurisdiction.

This needs direct evidence before making strong claims about legal sovereignty.

### Trading power

A continent-scale merchant organization would naturally accumulate:

- logistics;
- credit;
- warehouses;
- route intelligence;
- private security;
- political leverage;
- faster information than many governments.

Commerce creates intelligence even without formal spies.

### Magic

If magic is taught/professionalized, the sequel to “magic exists” is:

- education;
- regulation;
- specialization;
- military use;
- labor markets;
- prohibited practices;
- class/access questions.

Specific DragonSword answers remain open.

## Layer 3 — historical sediment

### The 60-year peace

A major war/crisis should leave:

- veterans;
- reconstruction;
- displaced families;
- abandoned defenses;
- memorials;
- commemorations;
- political careers;
- competing memories.

The current warm countryside can itself be evidence of successful recovery.

### Six Heroes as cultural property — **INFERRED C023-C024**

Once heroes become historical symbols, institutions can reinterpret them.

Possible claims:

- Crown: defenders of Orbis.
- Order: servants of divine purpose.
- mercenaries: adventurers like us.
- rebels: symbols captured by authority.
- historians: public story is simplified.

These examples are analytical possibilities, not canon positions.

### Missing sixth

The persistence of the phrase “Six Heroes” beside a reported five-person monument is exactly the kind of residue left when language outlives an edited public memory.

Strong clue; cause still UNKNOWN.

## Layer 4 — Dragon Dust economy/metaphysics

### Central knot — **INFERRED C022**

Dragon Dust appears to connect:

**religion**  
↕  
**historical victory**  
↕  
**resource/economy**  
↕  
**extraordinary power**  
↕  
**danger/corruption**  
↕  
**Dragons/monsters**

If primary evidence confirms this network, Dragon Dust may be the single best lens for understanding Orbis.

### “Using the corpse of the enemy”

If C011-C013 are confirmed directly, Orbis has a profound contradiction:

> the civilization venerates the defeat of Kalagon while materially benefiting from Kalagon's remains.

That creates likely cultural questions about purity, necessity, ownership and taboo.

Those arguments are inferred; their existence is not yet canon.

## Layer 5 — deep historical theories

### Kalagon's defeat did not end Kalagon's consequences — **SPECULATIVE C025**

Possible causal model:

```text
Kalagon defeated
→ remains enter world
→ Dragon-related material persists
→ civilization exploits it
→ new Dragon/power crises
```

Missing link: exact canonical relation among Kalagon remains, Dragon Dust and Origin Dragons.

### Recurring Dragon cycle — **SPECULATIVE C026**

Two major Dragon crises separated by a long peace could indicate:

- literal recurrence mechanism;
- consequences of Dragon material use;
- independent dragons repeating a historical pattern;
- narrative rhyme rather than in-world cycle.

Do not collapse these alternatives prematurely.

### Organa / Kalagon symmetry — **SPECULATIVE C027-C028**

The religious framing may simplify an ancient conflict between beings whose nature is still unknown.

Alternative: the simple good-goddess/evil-dragon account may be substantially accurate.

Need direct cosmology.

## Bottom — things the world is not telling us yet

High-value UNKNOWNs:

- What existed before the first human kingdom?
- What exactly were Organa and Kalagon?
- Why did they fight?
- What is an Origin Dragon?
- What is Dragon Dust physically/metaphysically?
- Why is Ropie missing from public commemoration?
- Who shaped the official Six Heroes narrative?
- What do ruins/Eona infrastructure predate?
- How old is the Dragon problem?
- Is the present crisis recurrence, consequence, resurrection or something else?

## Working synthesis

The strongest current possibility is not:

> a peaceful fantasy kingdom is unexpectedly attacked by a Dragon.

It is:

> **a functioning civilization lives downstream from ancient Dragon history, has normalized some of its consequences, remembers a more recent crisis through simplified heroic myth, and is now discovering that the buried causes were never fully resolved.**

That is an inference framework, not yet a final lore answer.
